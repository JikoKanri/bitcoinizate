How to play

  • Tap or press space to flap between the wicks.
  • Each candle pays cash. BUY BTC converts all cash; SELL BTC returns dollars.
  • Eating a bull pumps price. A bear or swan dumps it. Halving is the fat pump — grab it high or low when called.
  • Cold storage saves a hit. Ten colds become one multisig life. Laser eyes vaporize a bear and unlock perks (DCA, IAbud, jukebox…).
  • Options is live from the title screen: sound, tutorial, and more.
